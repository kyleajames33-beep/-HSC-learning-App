Key Angles

1 rad ≈ 57.3° — arc = 1 radius
π rad = 180° — semicircle
2π rad = 360° — full circle (≈6.283 radii)
π/2 rad = 90° — quarter circle

Definition: 1 radian is the angle subtended at the centre of a circle by an arc equal in length to the radius. Since the full circumference is 2πr, a full revolution = 2π radians.
