Hints

Amplitude a = (max − min) ÷ 2. Find the highest and lowest data points.
Period T = time between two peaks. Then b = 2π / T.
Vertical shift d = (max + min) ÷ 2. It’s the midline of the data.
Phase shift c = x-value of first peak (for sine). Adjust c until the peak of your curve aligns with the first data peak.
